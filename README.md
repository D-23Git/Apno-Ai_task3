## ✨ Key Features & Page Structure

### 1. Header & Sticky Navbar
* **Glassmorphism Blur:** Modern translucent background with dynamic shadow on scroll.
* **Theme Switcher:** Instant Dark Mode 🌙 / Light Mode ☀️ toggle with persistent `localStorage` preference.
* **Responsive Drawer:** Seamless mobile hamburger navigation menu with smooth toggle.

### 2. High-Converting Hero Section
* Punchy headline and warm bakery storytelling.
* Live trust counters: **4.9★ Google Rating**, **36-Hour Cold Fermentation**, **100% Preservative-Free**.
* Dual Action CTAs: *"Browse Today's Bakes"* and *"Pre-Order Loaves"*.
* Floating morning batch notification card.

### 3. About Us & Ancient Leavening Philosophy
* 7+ Years Heritage badge.
* Explaining wild yeast leavening culture (*Aurora*) and stoneground organic flours.
* 4 Quality checkmarks: Organic Flour, French Normandy Butter, Single-Origin Arabica Espresso, and Clean Baking.

### 4. Interactive Menu & Offerings (Bonus Feature 🌟)
* **Instant Category Filter Tabs:** Filter items by *All Items*, *Sourdough Breads*, *Viennoiserie & Pastries*, and *Specialty Coffee* without page reloads.
* Hand-curated product cards with prices, weights, allergen badges, and high-resolution visuals.
* **Quick "Add to Order" Helper:** Clicking any item auto-fills the reservation form and scrolls down smoothly.

### 5. Quality Standards & Promises
* 4 Commitment highlights: Oven-hot at 7:30 AM, 100% Organic Grains, Zero Food Waste policy, and Direct Trade Coffee.

### 6. Customer Testimonials & Social Proof
* 3 Real customer reviews with 5-star rating badges, reviewer avatars, and authentic Pune community quotes.

### 7. Interactive Pre-Order & Table Booking Form (Bonus Feature 🌟)
* Interactive form with date picker, inquiry dropdown, and client-side validation.
* Instant animated confirmation banner.
* **WhatsApp Direct Bridge:** 1-Click option to forward formatted reservation details directly to WhatsApp.

### 8. Floating WhatsApp Button
* Fixed bottom-right quick chat button with pre-filled greeting message.

### 9. Professional Footer
* Opening hours, location, navigation links, copyright, and verified student intern credit line.

## 💻 How to Run Locally

1. Clone or download this repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/D-23Git/Apno-Ai_task3.git
   ```
2. Navigate to the folder:
   ```bash
   cd Apno-Ai_task3
   ```
3. Open `index.html` directly in your browser:
   * Double click **`index.html`**, OR
   * Run with Node: `node server.js` and visit `http://localhost:3000`
