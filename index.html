Our Philosophy

Bread Made the Ancient Way — Flour, Water, Salt & Time.

The Daily Crust was founded with one simple promise: return to the roots of authentic baking. We never use commercial quick-rise yeasts, conditioners, or chemical improvers.

Every single loaf is nurtured with our 7-year-old sourdough starter ("Aurora"), undergoing a gentle 36-hour slow cold fermentation. The result is a crackling caramelized crust, an airy custardy crumb, and easy digestibility.
